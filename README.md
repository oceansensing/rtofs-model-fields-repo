# rtofs-model-fields-repo

NOAA's Global Real-Time Ocean Forecast System's scalar fields, as gridded data
for the oceansensing.org ocean map: sea surface height and sea ice
concentration and thickness, global at 0.25°, and over the US East Coast at
0.08° the surface temperature and salinity and the temperature and salinity
near the bottom, from a day behind to two days ahead. Its sibling,
`rtofs-model-currents-repo`, publishes the model's currents.

Updated four times a day.

## Sources and credits

NOAA's Global Real-Time Ocean Forecast System, from NOAA's open data on AWS, registry entry <https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-rtofs/>.
